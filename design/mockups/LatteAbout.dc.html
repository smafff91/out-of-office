HOW CERTIFICATION WORKS

Certified for remote work

A certified place is somewhere you can sit down with your laptop and work without anyone asking you to leave. It's not a ranking: it filters out the places where you can't really work, like standing-only bars or busy brunch spots.

To compare certified places, look at the score and the details on each page.

What a certified place has

Tables you can work at
Laptops welcome, with any limits made clear
e.g. "no laptops 13:00–15:30", shown on the page
Wi‑Fi for customers
Open during the day on weekdays

Always independent

Certification can't be bought. Places can't pay for the badge or for a better position. If a place stops meeting these criteria, it loses the badge.

Something changed at a certified place? Report it from the place's page. We review every report.
